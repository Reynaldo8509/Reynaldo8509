## Publication rule

The matrix records the strongest safe statement for each capability. It deliberately does not turn documentation, a service screenshot or a planned rule into a validated detection. New evidence should identify the source event, timestamp/window, effective rule, alert result, negative control and any Dashboard/index proof separately.
