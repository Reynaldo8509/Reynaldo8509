# Contributing

Contributions to the portfolio documentation and project material are welcome when they remain safe, accurate and reproducible.

1. Do not add credentials, private keys, tokens, backups, full private transcripts or screenshots that have not received visual review.
2. Preserve the documented distinction between the MANAGEMENT, ATTACK/LAB and NAT networks in the SOC Operations Lab.
3. Do not claim a detector is validated without real events and the evidence described in the relevant project documentation.
4. Keep technical claims scoped to their supporting evidence and state known limitations.
5. Use the pull-request template and explain validation performed for material changes.
